# Revision history

## Version 3: the verification paradox (current)
The paper was rebuilt around a single core argument after the authors judged version 2 to be a synthesis of familiar ideas. Core claim: generative AI makes verification expertise the scarce human input while eroding the entry-level work through which that expertise is formed (elimination and hollowing). Mechanism modelled with stocks (oversight capability, verification debt) and flows. Counterintuitive predictions: measured performance rises while risk accumulates (P1); early winners become later losers (P3); senior squeeze (P4); collective shortage through under-investment in transferable training (P5); deliberate expertise formation as moderator (P6); contrasting Asia–Pacific propositions for offshore-service, internal labour market and market-based systems (P7a–c). An illustrative simulation (Figure 2, `simulation.py`) shows the dynamics. Elements of version 2 retained: invisible verification work, reinforcing capability-trap logic.

Revised after the simulated review in `review_round1.md` (propositions made directional, Asia–Pacific configurations added, counter-evidence engaged). Superseded by version 3.
